# Analysis Framework Reference

Detailed analysis dimensions and scoring rubrics for competitive intelligence.

## Analysis Dimensions

### Messaging Analysis
- **Problem framing**: What pain points does the competitor highlight?
- **Value proposition**: Primary benefit claim and supporting proof points
- **Positioning statement**: How they differentiate from alternatives
- **Proof points**: Case studies, stats, testimonials, certifications

### Creative Analysis
- **Visual style**: Color palette, typography, imagery style
- **Ad formats**: Static, video, carousel, interactive
- **CTAs**: Primary and secondary calls-to-action, urgency language
- **Landing page structure**: Hero, social proof, features, pricing, FAQ

### Copy Formula Analysis
- **Headline structures**: Question / Stat / Benefit / PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
- **Tone**: Professional, casual, urgent, aspirational
- **Length**: Short-form vs long-form preference
- **Hooks**: Opening lines that drive engagement

### Audience Analysis
- **Segments**: Demographics, psychographics, firmographics
- **Channels**: Where they invest most (social, search, content, events)
- **Funnel stage**: Top (awareness), mid (consideration), bottom (conversion)
- **Geographic focus**: Markets and localization strategy

### Pricing Analysis
- **Model**: Freemium, free trial, demo-gated, usage-based, flat-rate
- **Tiers**: Number of tiers, feature differentiation, price anchoring
- **Competitive positioning**: Premium, mid-market, budget
- **Discounts**: Annual vs monthly, startup programs, volume pricing

### Social Media Analysis
- **Posting frequency**: Posts per week per platform
- **Engagement rate**: Likes + comments + shares / followers
- **Content themes**: Product updates, thought leadership, culture, community
- **Platform strategy**: Which platforms prioritized and why

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 5 | Excellent | Industry-leading, highly differentiated |
| 4 | Strong | Above average, clear strategy |
| 3 | Average | Competent but undifferentiated |
| 2 | Weak | Below average, gaps visible |
| 1 | Poor | Minimal effort or absent |
